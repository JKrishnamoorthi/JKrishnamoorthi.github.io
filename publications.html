Krishnamoorthi J

PhD Research Scholar, Aligarh Muslim University · Senior Research Fellow, Institute of Physics, Bhubaneswar

I work on neutrino oscillation phenomenology, with a focus on neutrino tomography of the Earth, non-standard neutrino interactions, long-range neutrino interactions, and detector analysis at large-scale neutrino experiments.
